# LearnQuick — Student guide

Your personal plan to reach **functional B1 Spanish** in 30–90 days. Everything runs on your Mac: no account, no internet needed, no tracking.

## Open it

1. Keep the `LearnQuick` folder somewhere safe (for example *Documents*).
2. Double-click **`LearnQuick.html`**. It opens in your browser (Safari works best).
3. Tip: drag `LearnQuick.html` to the right side of your Dock for one-click access.

The first time, a short setup asks your name, gives you a 15-question placement test and recommends a 30, 60 or 90-day plan.

## Your daily routine (25–35 min in the app + listening)

1. Open **Today's plan** (or press *Continue today* on Home).
2. Do the tasks from top to bottom. Usually:
   - **Review:** a few flashcards that are due.
   - **A lesson or a trainer:** verbs, fui vs. iba, el/la…
   - Saying a few phrases **out loud**.
3. Then get your **Spanish input** away from the app: a podcast on your commute, an episode of a series, or a language exchange. Log the minutes with *+15 / +30 / +60*.

You only need **10 XP** (one short session) to keep your streak. Finishing all of today's tasks is what moves your plan forward.

### Streak rules
- Weekends, US federal holidays and days off you add in *Settings* **never break your streak**.
- You can hold up to **2 streak freezes** (🛡). You earn one every 5 streak days, and another one any time you study on a day off. If you miss a study day, a freeze is used automatically.

## Small habits that make a big difference

- **Always learn the noun with its article:** *el mapa*, *la mano*. In the app, **el** is blue and **la** is pink.
- **Read every Spanish line out loud.** Exaggerate the RR. Keep vowels short and pure: “no” is not “noh-oo”.
- **Spanish subtitles, never English.** If it's too fast, slow it to 0.9×.
- **Don't know a word? Describe it in Spanish:** *Es una cosa que sirve para…*
- **Don't worry about the subjunctive yet.** Recognize it. Focus on **fui vs. iba**.

## Typing accents on the Mac

- **Hold down a vowel key** (for example `e`) and choose *é* from the menu.
- Or press **Option + E**, then the vowel → á é í ó ú. **Option + N**, then N → ñ.
- Below every answer box there are buttons for **á é í ó ú ñ ü ¿ ¡**.
- An answer that is only missing an accent counts as *almost right*.

## Make the voice sound great

The app uses the Spanish voices built into macOS. For a much more natural voice:

1. Open **System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content**.
2. Next to *System Voice*, choose **Manage Voices…**
3. Under **Spanish**, download an **Enhanced** or **Premium** voice (for example *Paulina*, Mexico).
4. Reopen LearnQuick, then go to **Settings → Voice** to pick it and press *Play a sample*.

## Daily reminder

Go to **Settings → Daily reminder**, choose a time and click **Add to Calendar**. This adds a repeating event on your study days to the Calendar app.

## Keep your progress safe

Your progress is saved in this browser on this Mac. **Once a week, click *Download backup*** (Home or Settings) and keep the file. If something is ever lost, open *Settings → Restore from a backup*.

If you clear Safari's website data, your progress goes with it, so download a backup first.

## Series you watch on Netflix

In the desktop app, the series you watch in Spanish on Netflix can count as listening: see [Count the Spanish you watch on Netflix](NETFLIX-GUIDE.md), with steps for Windows and Mac.

## Checkpoints and the B1 mock exam

- At the end of each phase, take a **15-question checkpoint** (pass with 70%). It tells you which lessons to review.
- At the end there is a **B1 mock exam** modeled on the official DELE B1: reading, listening, grammar, writing and 4 timed speaking tasks. Writing and speaking are partly self-graded, so be honest with yourself.

## Questions

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| No sound | Check *Settings → Voice*, download a Spanish voice (see above), and make sure your Mac isn't muted. |
| “Record me” doesn't appear | Your browser doesn't allow the microphone here. Use **Listen & repeat**: listen, then say it out loud 2–3 times. |
| I'm behind on my plan | No stress. Just do today's plan; the app shows your pace and new target date. You can also switch to a longer plan in *Settings*. |
| I want the app in Spanish | *Settings → Interface language → Español* (great for trick 2). |
